# SIGNALDESK
### AI Investment Committee Workspace

Four specialized analyst agents debate any stock in parallel.
**Bull · Bear · Macro · Quant** — all firing simultaneously via `asyncio.gather()`.

---

**How to use:**
1. Type any ticker in the search bar (e.g. `NVDA`, `RELIANCE.NS`, `AAPL`)
2. Click **ANALYZE →** or press Enter
3. All 4 analysts respond in under 3 seconds
4. Read the consensus meter and BUY/HOLD/SELL verdict

> ⚡ Powered by Groq `llama-3.3-70b-versatile` with multi-key rotation + Gemini fallback
